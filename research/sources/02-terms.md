# suno.com/terms (public, read 2026-11-04): clauses that shape how we may study the service

- Prohibits reverse engineering or attempting to discover source code.
- Prohibits scraping, data mining, robots or similar extraction methods.
- Prohibits obtaining content through means not intentionally made available.
- Prohibits circumventing content protections or blocks.
- Prohibits using the service to create, develop or improve competing products or services.
- Outputs may only be obtained through Suno's own download channels.

Consequence for this project: clean room only, public pages read by hand, no automated capture of
suno.com, no reading of its code or traffic, no use of its outputs as data.
