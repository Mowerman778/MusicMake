# suno.com landing page (public, read once on 2026-11-04)

Summary in our own words. No page source, scripts or network traffic were read.

- Navigation: Products (Studio, MCP, Voices, Stem Separation, Remix, Custom Models, iOS/Android apps),
  Resources (Music Library, AI Music Tools, Help Center), Log in / Join, Open App.
- Hero: a single prompt box as the entry point to "Create".
- Below the hero: a community showcase of user tracks and creator profiles, product tiles (Studio,
  Voices, Stem Separation, Remix, Custom Models, Music Library), feature highlights and an FAQ.
- Core promise: a complete song (vocals, lyrics, production) from a text prompt.
- Controls named publicly: style, exclusions, vocal gender, a "weirdness" slider and a style-influence slider.
- Studio: a browser-based multitrack editor for arranging, editing and mixing.
- Voices: sing new lyrics with a recorded sample of your own voice.
- Remix: change a track's style, lyrics or base recording.
- Custom Models: a model tuned on your own recordings.
