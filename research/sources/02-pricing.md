# suno.com/pricing (public, read 2026-11-04)

- Free: 50 credits per day, free models only (best is v6-mini), 4 concurrent generations, no downloads per
  month, no commercial rights, no stems, uploads up to 8 minutes.
- Pro: $8/month billed yearly, 2,500 credits/month, v6 and v6-wild, 20 song downloads/month, commercial
  rights, 2 stem-separation types, priority queue (10 concurrent), own-voice and custom models, uploads up
  to 30 minutes.
- Premier: $24/month billed yearly, 10,000 credits/month, adds Studio (MIDI, effects, automation, plugins),
  60 downloads/month, 3 stem-separation types, add vocals/instrumentals to existing songs.
- Credits per song are not stated on the page. Subscription credits don't roll over; bought credits don't
  expire but need an active subscription.
