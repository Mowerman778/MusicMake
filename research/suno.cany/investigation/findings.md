# Findings

- **fnd_1** The core job is one prompt in, a complete song with vocals, lyrics and production out; everything else (Studio, Voices, Remix, Stems, Custom Models) builds on a generated or uploaded track. (Seen it; evidence: ev_08a832f4737e9f011324add0afd1932e510c44dbc7089086d68ec3da4a9bfd77)
- **fnd_2** User controls named publicly are: style, things to leave out, vocal gender, an adventurousness-type slider and a style-influence slider, plus a simple prompt-only mode. (Seen it; evidence: ev_08a832f4737e9f011324add0afd1932e510c44dbc7089086d68ec3da4a9bfd77)
- **fnd_3** The Terms forbid reverse engineering, scraping, robots and building competing products, so only public, hand-read information is used and nothing is automated against suno.com. (Seen it; evidence: ev_b3ac72c5a6aab7a79b2cd0f06ab5db7f641ac3304602df5822fc938b8ee2631a)
- **fnd_4** ACE-Step 1.5 (MIT) covers text-to-song with vocals, remix (cover) and extend (repaint) through a local REST API on under 4 GB of VRAM; Demucs (MIT) covers stem splitting. (Seen it; evidence: ev_139a998f33b4ee253ceaf84275996bf30ec40320784cb338883c8a18fb411fdd)
- **fnd_5** For a private app, the jobs worth covering first are: prompt to song, lyrics to song, two takes, extend, remix, stems, download, library with favourites, and a lyric helper. (Worked it out; evidence: ev_08a832f4737e9f011324add0afd1932e510c44dbc7089086d68ec3da4a9bfd77, ev_139a998f33b4ee253ceaf84275996bf30ec40320784cb338883c8a18fb411fdd)
