# Findings

- **fnd_1** The core job is one prompt in, a complete song with vocals, lyrics and production out; everything else (Studio, Voices, Remix, Stems, Custom Models) builds on a generated or uploaded track. (Seen it; evidence: ev_7d4c5590c94411e26ce88f6b4ba252b852de408f6b9f907f3523affedd27b555)
- **fnd_2** User controls named publicly are: style, things to leave out, vocal gender, an adventurousness-type slider and a style-influence slider, plus a simple prompt-only mode. (Seen it; evidence: ev_7d4c5590c94411e26ce88f6b4ba252b852de408f6b9f907f3523affedd27b555)
- **fnd_3** Pricing is credit-based with a free daily allowance and two paid tiers; downloads, commercial rights, stem types, Studio and upload length are what separate the tiers. (Seen it; evidence: ev_9aa2e6e8d7234afb70b04fd187fe382a653dd7ffaabe657e5b912a3dfddf6cdc)
- **fnd_4** The Terms forbid reverse engineering, scraping, robots and building competing products, so only public, hand-read information is used and nothing is automated against suno.com. (Seen it; evidence: ev_0ed7b49a322a85cfef2227f15e76e5a99c4ed44579fb0a54f2e5963eb4e92836)
- **fnd_5** Recent direction (third-party coverage): personalisation (own voice, custom models, taste), a browser DAW (Studio), and a v6 model family with free and paid variants. (Best guess; evidence: ev_aeda28e90a1566472eb7b25bb863c7d2e34875b3afc89cb6d3c701195ec78ca9)
- **fnd_6** ACE-Step 1.5 (MIT) covers text-to-song with vocals, remix (cover) and extend (repaint) through a local REST API on under 4 GB of VRAM; Demucs (MIT) covers stem splitting. (Seen it; evidence: ev_62e5efca72852bf136a23d3f821f4b2d7ea1c869fa674f88ca1c17428710a281)
- **fnd_7** For a private app, the jobs worth covering first are: prompt to song, lyrics to song, two takes, extend, remix, stems, download, library with favourites, and a lyric helper. (Worked it out; evidence: ev_7d4c5590c94411e26ce88f6b4ba252b852de408f6b9f907f3523affedd27b555, ev_9aa2e6e8d7234afb70b04fd187fe382a653dd7ffaabe657e5b912a3dfddf6cdc, ev_62e5efca72852bf136a23d3f821f4b2d7ea1c869fa674f88ca1c17428710a281)
